# Credit Card team — CTA guidelines (2023)

> Deprecated. The Credit Card team adopted the design system button guidance in
> 2025. Kept for historical reference only.

## CTA labels

Write CTA labels in **Title Case**, for example "Activate Your Card". Keep CTAs
to two words where possible.

## CTA padding

Big CTAs use **24px** horizontal padding so they feel more prominent on the
card activation screen.

## Toasts

Confirmation toasts dismiss after **3 seconds**.
