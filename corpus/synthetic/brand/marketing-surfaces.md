# Brand — buttons on marketing surfaces

Marketing pages follow the design system for buttons, with two exceptions that
apply only to campaign and landing pages, never to in-product screens.

## Size

Use the large expressive button size for hero banners so the button balances
16px body copy.

## Labels

Campaign buttons may use a short brand phrase instead of verb + noun, but must
still use sentence case.
