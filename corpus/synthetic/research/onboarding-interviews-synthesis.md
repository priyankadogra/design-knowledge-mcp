# Onboarding interviews — research synthesis

Fourteen moderated interviews with new customers across the onboarding and loan
application flows.

## Finding 1: People hunt for "the big purple button"

Most participants described the main action as "the big purple button" and
looked for it at the bottom of the screen. When two equally prominent buttons
appeared side by side, six of fourteen hesitated or picked the wrong one.
Recommendation: keep a single high-emphasis action per screen.

## Finding 2: Approval messages vanish too fast

Nine participants tried to re-read loan approval amounts after the confirmation
toast had already disappeared. Several took screenshots to remember the number.
Recommendation: important financial confirmations should persist or stay longer
than routine toasts.

## Finding 3: Vague labels slow people down

Labels like "Continue" and "OK" caused people to stop and re-read the screen.
Labels that named the outcome, such as "Review offer", were understood faster.
